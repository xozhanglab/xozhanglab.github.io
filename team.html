Principal Investigator

Xiao-Ou Zhang PI

zhangxiaoou@tongji.edu.cn kepbod.com @kepbod 1239 Siping Road, Shanghai 200092

Xiao-Ou earned a B.E. in Biological Engineering from Huazhong Agricultural University and a B.S. in Computer Science from Huazhong University of Science and Technology in 2011. He received his Ph.D. in Computational Biology in 2016 from the CAS-MPG Partner Institute for Computational Biology (PICB), Shanghai Institutes for Biological Sciences (SIBS), Chinese Academy of Sciences. During his doctoral studies he focused on the biogenesis and processing of long noncoding RNAs with distinctive structures, working with Prof. Li Yang and Prof. Ling-Ling Chen. In 2016 he moved to UMass Chan Medical School as a postdoctoral fellow in the lab of Prof. Zhiping Weng, where he developed computational methods to study how repetitive sequences function in gene regulation. He joined the School of Life Sciences and Technology at Tongji University in 2020 as a professor and group leader. His research focuses on developing computational algorithms and mining large-scale high-throughput data to investigate gene regulation driven by noncoding sequences.
